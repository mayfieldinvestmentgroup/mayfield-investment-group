Who We Are

Financing built around qualifying investment opportunities.

Mayfield Investment Group Corp operates as a private direct lender serving clients across the United States. Founded in 2017, the company specializes in short-term fix-and-flip loans and long-term financing solutions for rental properties, tailored to the needs of real estate investors and other legitimate businesses.

These financing solutions, commonly referred to as private or hard money loans, can support the acquisition or refinancing of non-owner-occupied residential and commercial properties, renovation initiatives, construction projects, and bridge financing needs.

Mayfield Investment Group Corp works with a broad range of clients, including real estate professionals, commercial contractors, developers, and small business owners nationwide. Our flexible lending programs are designed to address investment property purchases, refinancing, renovation projects, construction, and transitional funding needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our financing.

What types of loans are available?

Programs include fix-and-flip, rental, multi-family, cash-out refinance, business, and construction financing, subject to applicable requirements.

What property types can be considered?

Programs may support qualifying residential and commercial properties, including investment and specialty-use properties and land where applicable.

Can you finance investment properties?

Yes, qualifying investment properties may be considered. Terms depend on the transaction, property, borrower information, collateral, documentation, and underwriting.

Do you offer construction financing?

Construction financing is available for qualifying projects, subject to project, collateral, borrower, documentation, and underwriting requirements.

How are loan terms determined?

Final terms are determined through the applicable review, underwriting, documentation, legal, property, and transaction requirements.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Website information does not constitute a commitment to lend or guarantee of approval. Applications are subject to review and final approval.

Can brokers submit opportunities?

Yes. Real estate professionals can review the broker program and submit qualifying opportunities through the broker registration process.
